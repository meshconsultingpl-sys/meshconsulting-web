Jobs for foreigners in Poland

Last reviewed: 1 November 2026

Areas with the most openings

These are the areas where there are usually the most openings. Specific jobs, with city and conditions, are in the vacancies section.

  • Warehouse and logistics: picking, packing, loading, forklift operator. They usually require neither language nor previous experience; rotating shifts. For forklifts some offers ask for a licence.
  • Production and factory: assembly lines, batteries, metal, food. Shift work, physical and repetitive.
  • Welding and metalworking: requires experience and sometimes a certificate. Better pay.
  • Construction and finishing: bricklayers, painters, installers.
  • Seasonal agriculture: fruit and vegetable harvests. Limited duration.
  • Hospitality and cleaning: hotels, kitchens, maintenance.

How to choose

  1. What you can do and what experience you can show.
  2. How many hours you want to work.
  3. Whether you need housing included.
  4. City and transport.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need experience?

For warehouse and production roles usually not; for welding and skilled trades, yes.

Are there jobs for women?

Yes, in logistics, production, sewing, cleaning and agriculture, depending on the offer.
