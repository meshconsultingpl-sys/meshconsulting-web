How much you earn working in Poland

Last reviewed: 1 November 2026

Minimum wage in 2026

Poland has a national minimum wage that is updated every year. In 2026 it is 4,806 PLN gross per month (about 3,600 PLN net, i.e. what reaches your account) and 31.40 PLN gross per hour for civil-law contracts such as umowa zlecenie. It is the legal floor: what you actually earn depends on the sector, your skills and the offer.

Gross and net

Contracts state gross pay (brutto). What you receive is the net amount (netto), after taxes and contributions. Deductions change with the type of contract (umowa o pracę / umowa zlecenie). Always ask for the estimated net pay.

What your income depends on

  • Hourly rate and hours worked per month.
  • Overtime and night or weekend shifts.
  • Type of contract.
  • Productivity or attendance bonuses, if the offer includes them.
  • Housing or transport: if deducted, it must be written in the contract.

Frequently asked questions

Why do some jobs pay more than others?

Because of the sector, the skills required and experience: a welder or an upholsterer earns more than an entry-level warehouse role.

Can I save money?

It depends on your pay, housing and expenses. Calculate with net pay, not gross.
