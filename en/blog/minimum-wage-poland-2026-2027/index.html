Minimum wage in Poland: 2026 and 2027

Published and checked: 24 November 2026
General information, not legal advice or a job offer. Last reviewed: 24 November 2026.

If you plan to work in Poland, it helps to know the legal minimum. There are two different figures: the monthly minimum wage and the minimum hourly rate for certain civil-law contracts.

Official figures (gross)

20262027
Monthly minimum wage (employment contract, umowa o pracę)PLN 4,806PLN 4,950
Minimum hourly rate (certain civil-law contracts, e.g. umowa zlecenie)PLN 31.40PLN 32.30

The 2027 figures are set by the Council of Ministers regulation of 14 September 2026 (Journal of Laws 2026, item 1213), in force from 1 January 2027.

Good to know

  • Figures are gross: net pay depends on contributions and taxes.
  • The hourly rate applies to certain civil-law contracts, not to an ordinary employment contract.
  • It is a minimum: each employer sets actual pay in its offer and contract.
  • This page does not promise earnings: what you receive depends on the employer, the hours and the type of contract.
