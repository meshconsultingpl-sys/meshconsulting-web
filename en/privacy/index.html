02Data we collect

We only process the data you give us through the website form, WhatsApp or email:

  • Candidates: full name, country of origin or nationality, phone / WhatsApp number, email address, the offer you apply for, area of interest, current location (Poland, EU or outside Europe), work experience and any documents you choose to send us (e.g. CV or education certificates).
  • Companies: company name, contact person, sector, number of workers needed and project details.
  • Communications: the content of the messages you exchange with us.

Providing your data is voluntary, but without the basic details (name, country, phone or email and consent) we cannot handle your application or contact you. We do not take decisions based solely on automated processing or build profiles with legal effects on you.

We do not request special-category data (health, religion, ethnic origin, etc.) through the website. If a vacancy requires a medical or sanitary certificate, it is handled directly with the employer.

04WhatsApp and automation

When you submit the form, your details are recorded in our candidate database (Google Sheets), we send you a confirmation email, and WhatsApp opens with a message about the offer you chose, which only reaches us if you send it. We use WhatsApp Business to reply to candidates and companies.

To reply faster we use automation tools (for example, n8n workflows with response assistants) that send offers and answer frequent questions. These tools do not make decisions with legal effects on you: the final selection of candidates is always made by a member of our team or by the employer.

You can ask us to stop sending you offers at any time by writing "STOP" on WhatsApp or by email.

09Cookies and storage

If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future (e.g. Meta Pixel), we will ask for your consent before enabling them and update this policy.
