About us

International recruitment with a legal contract and a personal touch

We are Mesh Consulting, a recruitment consultancy based in Wrocław, Poland. We connect people from Latin America, Asia and Africa with legal jobs in Poland and help Polish companies fill their vacancies.

02Mission and vision

MISSIONWhat we do

To connect international candidates with legal employment in Poland: explaining the conditions clearly before they travel and supporting them from application to their first day. And to help Polish companies fill their vacancies with motivated, well-prepared people.

VISIONWhere we are heading

To be an international recruitment consultancy that candidates and companies trust for its transparency, legal compliance and personal approach.

03How we work

STEP 1Application

You choose an offer and message us on WhatsApp or leave your details in the form.

STEP 2Selection

An advisor reviews your profile and confirms requirements, pay and schedule.

STEP 3Paperwork

We guide you through registration, permits and formalities.

STEP 4Arrival and work

Before you travel we explain what the offer includes (accommodation, transport, schedule) and support you on your first day.

04Our commitments

  • Legal contracts. Declared work with a contract.
  • Clear conditions from day one. Pay, schedule, contract type and accommodation are confirmed in writing before you travel or start work.
  • Protected data. We handle your data in line with GDPR. See privacy policy.
  • Support. A real person answers you on WhatsApp throughout the process.

06Frequently asked questions

How do I apply?

Choose an offer and leave your details in the website form or message us on WhatsApp at +48 797 325 805. An advisor will review your profile and reply.

Which languages do you support?

Spanish and English.

Where can I see the conditions of each offer?

On each vacancy page. Conditions come from the employer and may change; the final conditions are confirmed in writing before you travel or start work.

How do you protect my data?

We only use the data you give us to handle your application. You can ask for access, correction or deletion at any time. More details in the privacy policy.

I am a company and need staff. What should I do?

Leave your request on the business page.
