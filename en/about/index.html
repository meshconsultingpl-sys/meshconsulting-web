About us

Job offers and consulting on Poland withtransparency and a personal touch

We are Mesh Consulting, a company based in Wrocław, Poland that works in marketing, job-offer distribution and consulting on Poland. We are not an employment or recruitment agency and we are not an employer: we share job offers from employers in Poland, put interested people in touch with them and offer guidance about Poland.

02Mission and vision

MISSIONWhat we do

To connect international candidates with job offers from employers in Poland, explaining clearly the conditions each employer publishes before candidates decide to travel. And to help Polish companies reach motivated, well-prepared candidates by sharing their vacancies.

VISIONWhere we are heading

To be an international consultancy in job-offer distribution and information about Poland that candidates and companies trust for its transparency, legal compliance and personal approach.

03How we work

STEP 1Enquiry

You choose an offer and message us on WhatsApp or leave your details in the form.

STEP 2Information

An advisor answers your questions and shares the requirements, pay and schedule published by the employer.

STEP 3Paperwork

We give you general information about registration, permits and formalities in Poland. The employer handles the documents.

STEP 4Arrival and work

Before you travel we explain what the employer offer includes (accommodation, transport, schedule) and where to find official information.

04Our commitments

  • Legal contracts. Declared work with a contract.
  • Clear conditions from day one. Pay, schedule, contract type and accommodation are confirmed in writing before you travel or start work.
  • Protected data. We handle your data in line with GDPR. See privacy policy.
  • Support. A real person answers you on WhatsApp throughout the process.
