01Who we are

Mesh Consulting sp. z o.o. (KRS 0001275324 · NIP 8982339482 · REGON 54603382100000), based in Wrocław, Poland, is a company working in marketing, job-offer distribution and consulting on Poland.

Contact: mesh.consultingpl@gmail.com · +48 797 325 805.

02What we are and what we are not

Mesh Consulting is not an employment or recruitment agency and is not an employer. We do not hire people, we do not sign employment contracts with candidates and we are not a party to the employment relationship.

We share job offers published by employers in Poland, put interested people in touch with them and offer general information and guidance about Poland.

03The offers we publish

Offers come from employers. Pay, hours, accommodation, transport, requirements and other conditions are set by each employer and can change or close at any time without notice.

An offer shown on the website does not guarantee that the vacancy is still available. To confirm its status, message us on WhatsApp and we will share the information we have.

04Contract, documents and permits

The contract, the documents and, where applicable, the work permit or visa are handled directly between you and the employer and with the competent authorities. Deadlines and approval depend on the authorities and we cannot guarantee them.

We do not guarantee that anyone will be hired, or any specific pay, number of hours or accommodation.

05Costs

Mesh Consulting does not charge candidates for the job offers we share.

Be wary of anyone who asks for money in exchange for guaranteeing a job, a permit or a visa. Some offers include accommodation or other costs set by the employer; they are stated in each offer and you should confirm them in writing with the employer before travelling.

06Form and WhatsApp

When you submit the form or message us on WhatsApp you give us details that must be true and your own. We use them to reply to you about offers, as set out in our privacy policy.

We only share your details with the employer (or its intermediary) of the offer you are interested in if you expressly agree, separately.

07Illustrative figures and calculator

The income calculator and any figures on the website are illustrations based on published offers. They are not an offer, a commitment or a guarantee of income.

08Information and consulting

The information and guides on the website are general and indicative. They are not legal, immigration or tax advice. For your own case, check with the competent authorities or a professional.

09Liability

To the extent permitted by law, Mesh Consulting is not responsible for the conditions, acts or omissions of employers, or for decisions of the authorities. This does not limit the rights the law gives you as a consumer.

10Governing law and changes

These terms are governed by Polish law. We may update them and will always publish the current version on this page with its date. Questions: mesh.consultingpl@gmail.com.
